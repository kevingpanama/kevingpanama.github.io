Research interests
======
* Educational measurement and item response theory, including methods that estimate a student's proficiency with the fewest possible items.
* Validity and fairness of assessments and algorithmic systems; evaluation of AI and LLM-based tools used in education.
* Diagnosing learning gaps by combining item response theory with knowledge graphs; personalized learning pathways and learning analytics.
* Machine learning for education and the social sciences, including student motivation and gamification in virtual learning environments.
* Standardized assessment of student competencies to inform data- and evidence-based education policy.
