## About the course

The general-education mathematics course in the university's basic-skills program, focused on using algebra to solve problems with graphical and symbolic representations. Includes additional hours of virtual open laboratory.

## Topics

* Algebraic expressions with integer and rational exponents
* Polynomials: operations and factoring
* First- and second-degree equations
* Equations with rational and radical expressions
* Linear inequalities

This course is a prerequisite for MATH 1500 Precalculus.
