**Role:** Research Advisor (co-advised with Johel Batista) · Fundación Ayudinga

I co-advised a student team designing an educational tool for learning fractions that combines **gamification, cognitive training, spaced repetition and adaptive learning**.

The design was based on **working memory** and **math anxiety**, two factors that affect performance in mathematics. The project was presented at the *Jornada de Iniciación Científica (JIC)* at Universidad Tecnológica de Panamá.
