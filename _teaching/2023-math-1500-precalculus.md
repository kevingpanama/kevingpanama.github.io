## About the course

A five-credit course on functions that prepares students for calculus and for the discrete-structures sequence in computer science.

## Topics

* Linear, polynomial, rational, exponential, logarithmic and trigonometric functions
* Operations with functions and inverse functions
* Analytic trigonometry and complex numbers
* Systems of linear and nonlinear equations, and inequalities
* Matrices, determinants and polar coordinates

This course is a prerequisite for MATH 2251 Calculus I and COMP 2501 Discrete Computational Structures I.
