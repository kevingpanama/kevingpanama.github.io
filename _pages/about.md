I am a data scientist and Systems and Computing Engineer specializing in **psychometrics and educational measurement**. I am a **Fulbright Scholar** pursuing an M.S.Ed. in Qualitative and Quantitative Research Methodology at **Indiana University Bloomington**, with an emphasis on measurement, multivariate statistics and machine learning for the social sciences.

For the past ten years I have built data and learning systems at [Fundación Ayudinga](https://ayudinga.org), a nonprofit e-learning foundation in Panama, where I am Director of Technology and Educational Research. There I direct an R&D portfolio of 10+ projects in machine learning, knowledge management and gamification for education, lead research on the factors that influence learning, and designed the foundation's data governance strategy and cloud infrastructure.

I combine software engineering, statistical modeling and measurement to build and evaluate models and assessments that are **valid, reliable and fair**.

Research interests
======
* Psychometrics and educational measurement
* Machine learning and AI evaluation
* Learning analytics and educational technology
* Multivariate statistics and missing data analysis
* Data governance and education policy in Latin America
