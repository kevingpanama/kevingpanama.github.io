## About the course

An upper-level course in the video game design and development program on applying artificial intelligence to games. Students implement AI algorithms in a programming language and develop one or more games that integrate them. 30 hours of lecture and 30 hours of closed laboratory.

## Topics

* AI for agent behavior and decision-making
* Game strategy
* Pathfinding (route-finding) algorithms
* Speech recognition and synthesis
* Implementing AI algorithms and integrating them into a working game

## My teaching

* Developed the course materials and delivered lectures, practice sessions and tutorials.
* Assessed student progress through exams, assignments and projects, with individualized feedback and academic advising.
