## About the course

The second course of the discrete mathematics sequence for computer science majors, which continues from COMP 2501 (which I also taught, Fall 2023). 45 hours of lecture and laboratory.

## Topics

* Matrices applied to linear transformations
* Counting techniques and combinatorial analysis
* Principles of discrete probability
* Boolean algebra and Karnaugh maps to simplify Boolean functions and combinational circuits
* Relations and functions

* Developed the course materials and delivered lectures, practice sessions and tutorials.
