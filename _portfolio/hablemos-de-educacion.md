**Organization:** Jóvenes Unidos por la Educación, Panama

* **#HablemosDeEducación19** (Aug 2018 – May 2019): co-organized a citizen advocacy campaign that proposed **19 education policy measures** for Panama's 2019 general elections and set out to track how the next government carried them out.
* **Selection and recruitment system** (2018–2020): built the applicant selection and recruitment system for the *Latin American Citizen Action Laboratory* and its interactive virtual platform.
* **"The Role of Youth in the Future of the Panama Canal"**: co-organized the first and second editions with the Panama Canal Authority, where 400+ young people presented their views on the Canal's challenges and future.
* **AC7IVATE!** (2019–2021): mentored a socio-emotional learning program for students aged 11–12.
