## About the course

The first course of the discrete mathematics sequence for computer science majors: the logical and mathematical foundations used to reason about programs and to model computational problems. 45 hours of lecture and laboratory.

## Topics

* Propositional logic and rules of inference
* Set theory and the algebra of sets applied to computing problems
* Proof by mathematical induction
* Graphs and trees as representations of models
* Introduction to matrix theory

This course is a prerequisite for COMP 2502 Discrete Computer Structures II.
